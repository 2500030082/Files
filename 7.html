IMAGE DESCRIPTION
The Taj Mahal is a giant, glowing white marble tomb complex built in Agra, India, featuring a massive central dome, four tall corner towers, and a large formal garden.
The Colosseum in Rome is famous for hosting brutal gladiatorial combats, wild animal hunts, and massive public spectacles as the largest amphitheater ever built
Chichén Itzá is a famous ancient Maya city in Mexico featuring El Castillo, the Great Ball Court, and El Caracol.
The Great Wall of China was built by multiple Chinese dynasties over two thousand years—most notably started by Emperor Qin Shi Huang of the Qin dynasty and famously rebuilt in stone by the Ming dynasty. It was built primarily to defend China's northern borders against invasions and raids by nomadic tribes, and to control trade along routes like the Silk Road.
Embedded within a dramatic landscape at the meeting point between the Peruvian Andes and the Amazon Basin, the Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu is among the greatest artistic, architectural and land use achievements anywhere and the most significant tangible legacy of the Inca civilization.
Petra is very famous for its breathtaking rock-cut architecture, its status as one of the New Seven Wonders of the World, and its history as a rich trading hub.
Christ the Redeemer is famous for its massive scale, its unique Art Deco design, and its iconic mountaintop location overlooking Rio de Janeiro. It is also celebrated as one of the New Seven Wonders of the World and a powerful global symbol of Christianity and peace.
